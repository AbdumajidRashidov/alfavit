## Do / Don't

- **Do** send before 10:00 Tashkent; reply to any journalist within the hour; offer numbers (visitors, bot users) when asked, honestly.
- **Do** log every send and reply in the table above the same day.
- **Don't** CC outlets together, attach files, follow up more than once, or claim anything not in `press-kit.md`.
- **Don't** argue about the reform itself. Alfavit is neutral infrastructure for whatever the law says.

## When a piece runs

1. Thank the journalist by reply; ask nothing more.
2. Add the URL to `private/trackers.md` and to `metrics.md` → press mentions.
3. Post the article in your Telegram channel with one line of thanks (no UTM needed; it is their link).
4. Check Cloudflare Web Analytics → Referrers the next day; note the number in the weekly review.
